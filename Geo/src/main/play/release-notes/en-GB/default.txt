Version 1.2

• Nature view rebuilt to name the peaks you see: a clean horizon with N/E/S/W markers and a label on each visible summit.
• A min-altitude slider hides small hills; peaks below the horizon aren't shown.
• Offline areas are now peaks-only: smaller and quicker.
• New Magnetic Conditions card: what a geomagnetic storm is doing to your compass, your GPS and your chances of seeing the aurora. Alerts off by default.
• Fixed: the altitude graph keeps tracking when GPS drops.
