Version 1.3

• Nearby peaks are more reliable: Geo asks three OpenStreetMap servers instead of one, and a busy server no longer reads as "no peaks here".
• Built-in mountains and offline-area peaks show at once, even with no signal.
• The last peak list is remembered, marked "possibly outdated" until a fresh one arrives.
• If the altitude floor hides every peak, Geo says so and resets it in one tap.
• A failed offline download no longer saves an empty area.
